---
name: Beer badge threshold eligibility
description: How corrected beer counts affect earned tiers and announcements.
---

Beer Me tiers are actively earned only while the seasonal beer count meets their thresholds. Keep historical award records, but do not use them alone to unlock artwork, previews, or pending announcements when the count has fallen.

**Why:** During testing, a count reduced to seven still displayed all six tiers as earned because old awards and earned-tier progress remained from a higher count. Resetting acknowledgement timestamps also replayed those old events without a new threshold crossing.

**How to apply:** Check the current seasonal metric when reading tier status or pending notices. Reconcile progress after count corrections, suppress pending events above the current count, and allow a fresh announcement if the count later crosses a threshold again. An old award row is history, not proof that the current trigger is met.
