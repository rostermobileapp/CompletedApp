---
name: Century Club calendar-year attendance
description: How annual appearance counts treat games, scrimmages, and historical badges.
---

Century Club milestones count events in the January 1–December 31 calendar year, independent of league seasons. Count scorekeeper-confirmed attendance in completed games and approved player requests for past, non-cancelled scrimmages; scheduled or merely pending scrimmages do not count.

**Why:** Games have explicit attendance records, while scrimmages have no separate played/attendance record. An approved roster request after the scrimmage has happened is the closest available participation evidence. A league season is not the same as a calendar year.

**How to apply:** Determine the event's year from its stored event date, and keep annual awards and progress scoped by year so they reset on January 1. Preserve older award records as history but do not show them as current-year eligibility or replay old announcements. Time-based scrimmage completion requires evaluation on the normal pending-announcement check because no finalize-game event fires.
