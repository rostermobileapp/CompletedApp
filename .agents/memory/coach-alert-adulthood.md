---
name: Coach alert adulthood
description: Why eligibility alert recipients require separate adult evidence.
---

Eligibility alerts use the normal adult boundary of 18, not Family setup's
separate 21+ boundary. Missing or malformed recorded DOB excludes a recipient
from these alerts; approved coaching authority alone is not age evidence.
This exclusion does not revoke their role or change unrelated staff operations.

**Why:** The alert requirement explicitly limits delivery to adult staff.
Failing closed avoids accidentally notifying a minor account with a historical
or mistaken coaching assignment. Reusing Family eligibility would incorrectly
exclude authorized adult coaches aged 18–20.

**How to apply:** Preserve independent exact-team authority, adult evidence and
selected-account rollout checks when adding delivery channels. Revisit missing
DOB handling through an explicit product decision rather than silently treating
every approved coach as an adult.
