---
name: Custom team awards scope
description: Confirmed captain-only scope for recurring custom team awards.
---

Use existing team captains for all Custom Team Awards management and grants. Assistant-captain support is explicitly deferred.

**Why:** On 2026-10-23, the user chose “Use existing captains only; defer assistant-captain support” when asked how to reconcile the uploaded brief with the lack of a separate assistant-captain role.

**How to apply:** Do not add an assistant-captain designation or grant-only role as part of this feature unless the user later expands the scope. This choice overrides the brief's assistant-captain permission requirement.

Award setup and management belong on the My Team page.

**Why:** The user explicitly reaffirmed that the setup must be in “My Team.”

**How to apply:** Keep creation and configuration accessible from My Team for the selected team, not exclusively in Trophy Case, Home, or admin screens.

Do not offer background removal for custom team award artwork.

**Why:** The user explicitly removed this feature because the external provider account, setup and cost were not worth it.

**How to apply:** Keep ordinary image upload/save, including already-transparent PNGs, without adding a background-removal provider or requiring provider credentials. Do not reintroduce the feature unless requested.

The entire My Team Custom Team Awards section must start minimized and expand only on an explicit click.

**Why:** The user said the always-expanded section was "WAY TOO BIG" and requested a minimized dropdown for the entire section, not merely the award list.

**How to apply:** Keep settings, descriptions, counts and management controls inside the disclosure; do not automatically open it as data loads or changes.

Custom team awards are transferable current-holder recognition, not permanent Trophy Case progression. Paid-tier gating remains undecided.

**Why:** The supplied product brief defines one holder per award, recurring periods, preserved history, and a feature flag rather than a chosen subscription policy.

**How to apply:** Do not convert custom grants into permanent patch unlocks or introduce a paid-tier requirement without a new product decision.

Preserved award history includes earlier holders within the same period, not only the latest recipient for each period.

**Why:** Updating the authoritative period record on reassignment would otherwise erase a valid recognition; historical recipients, captains and timestamps must remain visible after archiving.

**How to apply:** Keep immutable recognition history separate from the mutable current-holder record. A no-op retry must not create another event.
