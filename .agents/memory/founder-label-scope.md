---
name: Founder label scope
description: Product intent for the exclusive Founder title on the Wall.
---

The Founder title is an exclusive, display-only exception for the designated founder account, not a new league role or a customizable title feature.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested that this apply to just that account and no other user.

**How to apply:** Keep existing authorization and subscription roles intact. Do not extend the title to other commissioners or infer founder status from league ownership.
