---
name: Hat Trick season history
description: How season-scoped Hat Trick awards relate to older career-scoped records.
---

Hat Trick thresholds are per season, regardless of calendar-year boundaries. Each completed game with at least three goals by the player adds one tally, not one tally per goal. Older career-scoped awards are historical records only; they must not advance the progress or tiers shown for any particular season.

**Why:** Earlier releases pooled qualifying games across seasons, so treating those older global awards as current-season progress could incorrectly unlock a new season's tier.

**How to apply:** Show a season's live count and tier eligibility using games assigned to that season. Keep legacy career awards visible as separate history, and never use them as evidence for season-scoped announcements.
