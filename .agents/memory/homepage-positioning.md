---
name: Homepage positioning
description: The emotional benefit that should lead Roster homepage design work.
---

Lead the homepage with enjoying time off the ice together, rather than spending that time on team admin.

**Why:** The user approved the initial scroll concept as a start, then emphasized that the main benefit is time with each other, not merely less administration or more playing time.

**How to apply:** Put the teammate-togetherness message before management features in homepage concepts. Keep preview work isolated until the user asks for live integration.

Position the hero headline high beneath the navigation, with its supporting content following as a group.

**Why:** The user clarified that “higher up on the page” means visibly higher page placement, not simply a smaller headline margin.

**How to apply:** Adjust the hero's top spacing when changing this placement, and verify the navigation does not cover the headline on desktop or mobile.
