---
name: Iron Man game streak
description: Eligibility, resets, and permanent tier history for consecutive game attendance.
---

Iron Man uses the current consecutive run of completed, non-scrimmage games a player was due to play. Missed games reset the counter to zero, even across seasons and calendar years. Tiers already earned remain earned; the longest historic run can establish tiers during a silent backfill without replacing the current counter.

**Why:** Counting all attended games treated nonconsecutive appearances as a streak. Resetting earned tiers on a miss would erase an award the player genuinely achieved. Team participation can be recorded through either direct team or league-assigned membership; substitutes and historical appearances can be represented by an RSVP or recorded game stat even without that membership.

**How to apply:** Treat a completed game as eligible if the player belonged to a participating team when it occurred, had a game RSVP, or has recorded participation. Only confirmed attendance or recorded participation proves a game was played; an RSVP marked attending does not. Exclude scrimmages and never scope the count to a season or year.
