---
name: League stats roster requirement
description: Product rule limiting league statistics to players on that league's teams.
---

League statistics are for players on teams, not every approved league member. Apply this to skaters, goalies, 3 Stars, league playoff stats, and league-scoped individual stats. Placeholder and imported players must also have a team in the league.

**Why:** The owner explicitly stated: "Unless a member of a league is on a team... that user should NOT show up on any stats for the league. Stats are for users on a team."

**How to apply:** Count valid league team assignments and approved direct roster memberships, but not pending team requests or teams outside the league. This is a visibility rule: preserve stored statistics and awards when a player becomes unassigned. Personal career history is separate from league listings.
