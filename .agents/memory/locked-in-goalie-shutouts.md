name: Locked In goalie shutouts
description: Goalie-only eligibility and tier-name transition for lifetime shutout badges.
---

Locked In is only visible and awardable for users with goalie evidence. Count completed non-scrimmage games across the user's entire career only when both scores are final, the opponent scored zero, and the player was either the recorded goalie of record or a confirmed attendee marked as a goalie on that team when no goalie-of-record row exists.

**Why:** A default zero in a goalie record is not proof of a shutout, and goalie records can be missing from historical completed games. Skater attendance must not produce goalie progress. Older membership timestamps may postdate confirmed historical attendance. The supplied artwork calls the 10-shutout tier Platinum and the 25-shutout tier Emerald, unlike the former generic Gold/Platinum catalog.

**How to apply:** Never reset by season or year. Gate evaluation, normal Trophy Case visibility, and pending earned announcements by goalie eligibility. Preserve older awards while mapping their tier names to the labels on the supplied patches; a catalog preview may still show all badges for review.
