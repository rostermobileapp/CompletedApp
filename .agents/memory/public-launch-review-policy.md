Hockey is live on the App Store and Google Play. Soccer and baseball have not launched; their pages should remain reachable but excluded from the sitemap and marked noindex in both HTML and response headers.

**Why:** The owner explicitly confirmed the live hockey launch and unlaunched status of the other sports.

**How to apply:** Do not restore dated launch announcements or imply soccer/baseball availability. Preserve public build-time HTML and llms.txt when making marketing edits.

Do not add placeholder customer reviews or unsupported ranking claims. The owner requested removing the sport/segment testimonial blocks until real, attributed quotes are supplied, and replacing the hockey “#1” claim with “Built specifically for hockey teams and leagues.”

**Why:** Marketing claims and quoted reviews must be supported by real evidence.

**How to apply:** Require supplied, attributable quotes before adding replacement testimonials; do not generate customer identities, star ratings, or endorsements.
