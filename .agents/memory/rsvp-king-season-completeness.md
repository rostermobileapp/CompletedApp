---
name: RSVP King season completeness
description: Season award timing and eligibility for a perfect RSVP response rate.
---

Award RSVP King separately for each ended league season when a player has explicitly answered Yes or No for every eligible non-scrimmage game. Include rostered games with no RSVP in the denominator and explicit substitute RSVPs among eligible games. A season with zero eligible games cannot qualify. Historical winners should be seeded without announcements; new season winners can be announced.

**Why:** Counting only submitted RSVPs rewards missing responses. Awarding before season end risks a later missed game. Unlike attendance or score-based awards, this badge measures answering invitations, so it does not depend on scorekeeper game completion.

**How to apply:** Use the season-scoped award as the unit of repetition and count earned seasons in its multiplier display; do not use the former first-response streak rule or count scrimmages.
