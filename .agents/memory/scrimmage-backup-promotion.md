---
name: Scrimmage backup promotion
description: Defines how organizer-approved backup players fill roster vacancies.
---

For Manual Approval and Pay to Play scrimmages, when an approved player is removed or withdraws before the scrimmage starts, the first active player on the organizer-managed backup list is automatically moved to approved. Do not require the backup player to accept the vacancy again. First to RSVP scrimmages never use a backup queue.

Full Manual Approval and Pay to Play rosters must continue accepting new RSVPs into the backup queue, including after roster finalization. Manual Approval queues the RSVP immediately; Pay to Play queues it after payment is confirmed.

**Why:** The organizer already approved and ranked the backup list. A withdrawal should fill the open roster spot immediately and tell the promoted player that someone dropped out and they are now in.

**How to apply:** Hide and reject backup actions for First to RSVP. For other modes, never reject an eligible RSVP solely because the approved roster is full; queue it, preserve ordering, and notify. Promote position 1 atomically after a vacancy.
