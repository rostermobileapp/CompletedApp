---
name: Season patch statistics
description: What the season's Patches Earned statistic represents.
---

Patches Earned counts all patch categories earned during the selected season's duration, including lifetime and trivia patches earned during that period; it is not limited to awards carrying that season's ID.

**Why:** The owner requested "all the patches earned during the duration of the season for the stats." Global and trivia awards do not necessarily have a season assignment.

**How to apply:** Use the award's earning timestamp and league-local season calendar dates, including the full final day. Treat each earned tier as a patch. All Seasons removes the date restriction. If dates are missing, use the season's schedule; an ongoing season with a known start can run through today. Do not substitute lifetime totals for a season with no known start.

Repeat-patch cumulative totals represent snapshots, not additional patches. The season's increase must subtract the highest total from before its start.

**Why:** Adding cumulative snapshots would count existing patches again. Announcements may also replay after corrected progress, without a newly earned patch.

**How to apply:** Count persisted awards, not announcement deliveries or the numeric goal/beer/trivia progress that qualified a tier.
