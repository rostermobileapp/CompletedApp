---
name: Sub badge eligibility
description: What constitutes a first-time substitution for the Sub patch.
---

Award the Sub patch once when a substitute request for a game is fully approved for a different player. Seed existing approvals silently and announce new ones.

**Why:** Full approval adds the substitute to the game schedule as attending and is the app's established record of filling a sub spot. An initial request, a team event without a game, or an RSVP alone does not establish that the player took another player's place.

**How to apply:** Use the final approved replacement assignment, not the approval of an intermediate stage. Keep the one-time award idempotent if approval processing or event polling retries.
