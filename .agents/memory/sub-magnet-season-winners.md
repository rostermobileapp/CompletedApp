---
name: Sub Magnet season winners
description: When to finalize the league-season No-RSVP leader award and how to treat ties and history.
---

Award Sub Magnet to every player tied for the most No RSVPs in a league season, only after that season has ended and at least one player has a No. Count a game once per player even if they have multiple team RSVP rows; exclude scrimmages.

**Why:** A live leaderboard can change with every RSVP, so awarding during an active season would issue irreversible patches to players who later stop leading. Previously completed seasons should not replay old achievement announcements.

**How to apply:** Keep awards scoped to the league season; seed historical winners silently, and announce new winners when a season closes or reaches its end date.
