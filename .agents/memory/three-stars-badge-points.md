---
name: 3 Stars badge points
description: Why 3 Stars badge eligibility uses weighted career points and how historical eligibility is handled.
---

The 3 Stars badge is a career achievement based on weighted points: first star earns 3, second earns 2, third earns 1. The season leaderboard shows the same scoring within a selected season, but badge tiers accumulate points across seasons.

**Why:** A player visibly had 5 leaderboard points from fewer than five nominations, while the Bronze 5 badge was incorrectly evaluated using nomination count. A points-based tier should be earned at five points.

**How to apply:** Use the same weights for live Trophy Case progress and automatic evaluation. Reconcile previously eligible tiers as historical awards without replaying old earned announcements; normal future threshold crossings still announce as they happen.
