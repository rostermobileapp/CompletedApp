---
name: Trophy Case depth direction
description: Project-specific feedback on how physical depth should read in the Trophy Case.
---

Depth should come from a stepped hierarchy of physical surfaces—outer frame, recessed panels, deeper group wells, and individually framed badge bays—with a consistent upper-left light source. Do not rely on inset shadows as the only cue.

**Why:** The user explicitly said the shadow-only depth treatment looked low-quality. The visual goal is for each nested card layer to appear farther into the screen, not merely darker or more shaded.

**How to apply:** When refining the Trophy Case or extending its depth language elsewhere, use edge geometry, contrasting surface planes, and visible rims first; use shadows as supporting detail.

Earned badge images should appear on a fully transparent wrapper with no extra circular outline, backplate, or medallion shadow. Locked or image-less placeholders can still use framing.

**Why:** The user specifically rejected the thin red outline around a badge image; the badge art itself should be the only focal point.

**How to apply:** Keep decorative depth on the containing card, not directly around an earned badge image. Do not reintroduce a generic ring when adjusting badge artwork styles.
